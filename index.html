Information about this dashboard

This interactive dashboard brings together a range of statistics about life in Northern Ireland, covering social, economic and environmental indicators.



Most statistics are sourced from the Northern Ireland Statistics and Research Agency (NISRA). Where suitable NISRA data are not available, data from other official sources are used.



Links alongside each chart allow you to:

  • view and download the source data.
  • find further information, including contact details for the data producer.
